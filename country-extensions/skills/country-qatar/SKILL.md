## 1. Regulatory Hierarchy

Qatar operates a **centralised national framework** (no emirate/state subdivisions). A single set of national instruments applies countrywide, supplemented by master-developer design guidelines in the large planned districts.

| Authority | Scope | Key Instruments |
|---|---|---|
| **Ministry of Municipality (MME, "Baladiya")** | Zoning, land use, building permits, planning | National building permit; zoning regulations; Qatar National Master Plan (QNMP) / Qatar National Development Framework 2032 |
| **Public Works Authority (Ashghal)** | Technical specifications, public buildings, infrastructure, drainage | **Qatar Construction Specifications (QCS 2014)**; Ashghal design manuals; road & drainage standards |
| **Qatar Civil Defence Department (QCDD, MOI)** | Fire & life safety, occupancy approval | QCDD Fire Safety Handbook (NFPA-based); fire-system NOC and final approval |
| **Kahramaa (QGEWC)** | Electricity, water, energy/water conservation | Kahramaa regulations; Qatar Construction (E&W) standards; **Tarsheed** conservation programme |
| **GORD (Gulf Organisation for Research & Development)** | Sustainability certification | **GSAS / QSAS** rating system |
| **Ministry of Public Health (MOPH)** | Healthcare facility licensing | Healthcare facility guidelines |

### 1.1 Master-Developer Layer
Large planned districts impose their own design review guidelines (DRGs) on top of national codes — comply with BOTH:
- **Lusail City** — Qatari Diar / QDVC; Lusail-specific development control regulations, district cooling mandate.
- **The Pearl-Qatar & Gewan Island** — United Development Company (UDC).
- **Msheireb Downtown Doha** — Msheireb Properties; very high sustainability bar (LEED + GSAS).
- **Education City** — Qatar Foundation design standards.
- **Free zones (Ras Bufontas, Umm Alhoul)** — Qatar Free Zones Authority (QFZA).
- Typical hierarchy: National code (QCS / QCDD / Kahramaa) → MME zoning → Master-developer DRG → plot brief.

## 2. Key Codes & Standards

- **QCS 2014 (Qatar Construction Specifications)** — the single master technical specification used across Qatari construction, issued by Ashghal. Multi-part (earthworks, concrete, structural steel, masonry, finishes, MEP, drainage, landscaping, etc.). It is a *specification*, not a building code in the IBC sense; it references international standards heavily. [VERIFY current edition — QCS 2014 is the long-standing baseline; check for newer amendments.]
- **Structural design** — Qatar practice follows **British Standards (BS)** and increasingly **Eurocodes (BS EN)**, with **ASCE 7** used for loads on some international projects. Concrete to BS 8110 / BS EN 1992; durability is critical (see §6). [VERIFY designer agreement on code basis per project.]
- **Reference codes by adoption** — IBC, ASHRAE 90.1 / 62.1 / 55, NFPA suite, and BS/Eurocodes are all used by reference; the governing set is fixed per project in the design basis.
- **Accessibility** — Qatar Accessibility / Universal Design guidance (see §4).

## 3. Fire & Life Safety (QCDD)

- Governed by the **QCDD Fire Safety Handbook**, which is **NFPA-based** (NFPA 101 Life Safety Code, NFPA 1, NFPA 13, NFPA 72, etc.) with Qatar-specific amendments. [VERIFY current Handbook edition with QCDD.]
- QCDD approval is a **mandatory gate**: design-stage approval, then inspection and a final **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** before occupancy.
- Fire-system contractors and consultants must be **QCDD-registered/approved**.
- Expect NFPA-style requirements: occupant-load-based egress, travel distance limits, fire compartmentation, sprinklers for most occupancies above threshold areas/heights, addressable detection, smoke control for atria and high-rise, fire command centres in tall buildings.
- High-rise (Qatar has many towers in West Bay/Lusail): stair pressurisation, firefighting lifts, refuge floors per QCDD high-rise provisions. [VERIFY thresholds.]

## 4. Accessibility / Universal Design

- Qatar has a strong inclusive-design agenda (Qatar National Vision 2030; legacy of accessible-venue standards from the 2022 FIFA World Cup).
- **Qatar Accessibility / Universal Design guidance** and the **Mada (Qatar Assistive Technology Center)** inform accessible-environment requirements; FIFA 2022 venues followed enhanced accessible-stadia guidance. [VERIFY the current mandatory accessibility code/handbook MME enforces — Qatar references international best practice (ADA / ISO 21542) plus local guidance.]
- Practical baseline: accessible routes, ramps ≤1:12, accessible WCs, lift provision, tactile/visual wayfinding, accessible parking — design to ISO 21542 / ADA dimensions absent a stricter local figure.

## 5. Energy & Sustainability

- **GSAS (Global Sustainability Assessment System)**, formerly **QSAS**, by **GORD** — the regional green-building rating system. **Mandatory for government buildings and many large/master-planned developments**; rated in stars/certification levels across categories (energy, water, materials, indoor environment, site, etc.). [VERIFY current GSAS version and the project-type threshold that triggers mandatory certification.]
- **Kahramaa** energy & water efficiency requirements and the **Tarsheed** national conservation programme set targets for building energy/water performance, efficient fixtures, and demand reduction.
- **District cooling** is the norm for large developments (Lusail, Pearl, Msheireb) — coordinate ETS rooms, no on-plot chillers.
- Energy modelling typically to **ASHRAE 90.1** baseline; many projects also pursue **LEED** alongside GSAS (especially Msheireb, Education City).

## 6. Structural, Geotechnical & Climate Overlays

- **Seismicity: low.** Qatar is far from major faults; wind and durability usually govern over seismic, but confirm site-specific seismic per the project code basis. [VERIFY seismic basis — typically low PGA; some projects still apply nominal seismic detailing.]
- **Climate: hot-arid Gulf.** Extreme summer temperatures (>45 °C), high coastal humidity, intense solar load — drives high SHGC control, shading, envelope insulation, and oversized cooling. Design cooling for very high ambient.
- **Geotechnical durability is a defining issue:** shallow, aggressive **sabkha** soils and **high-sulfate, high-chloride groundwater** near the coast. Specify sulfate-resisting / blended cements, low water-cement ratios, adequate cover, and waterproofing/tanking to QCS durability provisions. Ground-gas and salt attack are routine concerns.
- **Wind:** design to the project code basis (BS EN 1991-1-4 or ASCE 7); no cyclones, but high open-terrain wind speeds.

## 7. Approval Workflow (typical)

1. **Land / zoning confirmation** — MME zoning, plot ratios, setbacks, height limits (district-specific; master-developer DRG if applicable).
2. **Design & building permit** — submit to MME (Baladiya) for the relevant municipality; architectural/structural/MEP review.
3. **QCDD fire approval** — design-stage fire approval, then construction and inspection, then fire **NOC**.
4. **Kahramaa** — electricity & water connection approval; energy/water compliance (Tarsheed).
5. **GSAS** — design and construction certification where mandatory (government/large projects).
6. **Other**: Ashghal (drainage/road interface), MOPH (healthcare), Ministry of Education (schools), Civil Aviation (height near airport), district cooling provider agreement.
7. **Completion / occupancy** — final inspections and certificate.

> Consultants and contractors generally must be **registered/classified** with the relevant Qatari authorities (MME, QCDD, Kahramaa). Confirm registration early.
